LEDGERLINE - QUICK START
========================

WINDOWS
  1. Install Python once (skip if you already have it):
       https://www.python.org/downloads/
     In the installer, TICK the box "Add python.exe to PATH".
  2. Double-click  start.bat
     The first time it needs 2-5 minutes and internet to prepare itself.
     Later it starts in seconds.
  3. Your browser opens. Create your administrator account, then fill in
     your business name, currency and logo.

MAC
  Double-click  start.command   (if the Mac blocks it: right-click > Open)

LINUX
  Run  ./start.sh

TO STOP THE APP
  Close the black window.

IMPORTANT - YOUR DATA
  * Your invoices live in the "data" folder.
  * Your private keys live in the file ".env".
  Copy BOTH to a safe place regularly (a USB drive or cloud folder).
  Without ".env" saved client details cannot be read.

NEED HELP?
  Open "Help & Guide" inside the app.
